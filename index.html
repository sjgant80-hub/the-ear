Sovereign & honest. Pure kernel, zero-dep, offline — no network, no cloud. The tones you hear are the exact 6-harmonic spectrum the gate scores (28/28): the gated logic is the live logic.
It makes a system's health hearable — it does not find bugs for you. The novel, real part: dissonance comes from the interaction of the voices, so a correlated fault is audible while every single metric is still green (try error storm and watch the eye stay green while the ear goes tense). Roughness model: Plomp–Levelt / Sethares. Built on the-throat. Code: github.com/sjgant80-hub/the-ear.
